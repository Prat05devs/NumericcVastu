# Running the partner (influencer / affiliate) programme

1. **Settings → Partner programme:** hold days (≥ your return window), minimum payout, terms version + page; TDS rate/threshold **only as advised by your accountant** (blank = no deduction).
2. **Pages:** write the partner terms (disclosure #ad/#paidpartnership, no outcome promises, no brand-name ad bidding, self-purchases earn nothing), get it reviewed, publish it.
3. **Partners → Invite:** name, email, commission (% or fixed), attribution window (default 30 days). Optionally create a coupon and "lock it to its link".
4. The partner signs in at `/partner` with that email, accepts the terms, copies links (or creates their own), and watches visits, orders, earnings and payouts. They never see customer details.
5. Commissions: pending → automatically approved after delivery + hold → included in a payout. Refunds reduce them; self-referrals are rejected automatically. Review flagged ones in the ledger.
6. **Partner payouts:** create a statement from the available balance, pay by bank/UPI, record the transfer reference.
7. Suspend a partner immediately if terms are broken; their links stop attributing.
